About the Audio Converter

Convert and compress audio between the formats that matter — entirely on your device. Feed in WAV, MP3, M4A/AAC, OGG, FLAC or WebM audio and export MP3 at the bitrate you choose, from transparent 320 kbps down to voice-grade 64 kbps for podcasts and dictation. A size estimate updates as you move the dial.

The heavy lifting is done by a pure-JavaScript MP3 encoder (lamejs) plus the browser's own audio decoder, so files are processed in memory and handed straight back. Voice recordings, lecture captures and demo tracks convert in seconds — and confidential audio is never uploaded to a converter site.

Frequently asked questions

What bitrate should I choose for MP3?

320 kbps is transparent for music, 192–256 kbps is excellent for everyday listening, and 96–64 kbps is ideal for spoken voice. The tool's size badges show exactly what each choice costs.

Can I convert M4A or FLAC to MP3?

Yes. Any format your browser can decode — including M4A/AAC, FLAC and OGG — can be re-encoded to MP3 locally.

Why convert WAV to MP3 at all?

MP3 files are typically 5–10× smaller than WAV with negligible quality loss at higher bitrates — much easier to email, upload to forms or store.

Are my recordings uploaded?

Never. Decoding and encoding run entirely in your browser. There is no server to receive them.
