About the Audio Trimmer

Cut the exact piece of audio you need — on a real waveform, not by guessing numbers. Load an MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC or WebM file, scrub through the actual shape of the sound, drag the selection handles until the range is razor-sharp, and export just that slice as WAV or MP3. Everything is decoded and encoded locally with the Web Audio API.

This is the tool for ringtone clips, podcast excerpts, voice-memo trimming, sampled sound effects and voice-note editing. Because the file is decoded in your browser with the Web Audio API, private recordings — interviews, meetings, voice notes — never get uploaded to a cutter website that keeps them.

How to use it

  1. 1

    Load your audio

    Drop an MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC or WebM file — the waveform draws instantly.

  2. 2

    Scrub to find the moment

    Navigate the waveform and play the region to locate your cut points.

  3. 3

    Set the range

    Drag the selection handles until start and end are exactly where you want them.

  4. 4

    Export the clip

    Save the selection as WAV (lossless) or MP3 — encoded locally, downloaded immediately.

Frequently asked questions

How do I trim an MP3 without losing quality?

Export the trimmed clip as WAV for a bit-perfect result, or as MP3 at 320 kbps for a tiny quality step that is inaudible in practice. Either way the cutting happens locally — no server re-encoding.

Which audio formats are supported?

Anything your browser can decode: MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A/AAC and WebM audio. The exported clip is WAV or MP3.

Can I make a ringtone with this?

Yes — that is a classic use. Load the song, select the 20–30 seconds you want, and export as MP3. The whole job takes under a minute.

Is my audio file uploaded anywhere?

No. Decoding, waveform drawing, playback and encoding all happen in your browser via the Web Audio API. Disconnect from the internet and it still works.
