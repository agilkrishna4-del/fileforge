About the CSV to Excel

Turn raw CSV files into real Excel workbooks — locally, instantly, free. The converter parses your CSV (including quoted fields, custom delimiters and escaped characters), detects number and date columns, and writes a proper .xlsx workbook with typed cells instead of text-that-looks-like-numbers. The result opens in Excel, LibreOffice, Numbers and Google Sheets behaving exactly as it should.

Anyone who has fought Excel's CSV import wizard — leading zeros eaten, dates mangled, everything left as text — knows why this tool exists. And because SheetJS builds the workbook inside your browser, customer exports, financial CSVs and internal data files are never uploaded to a converter site.

Frequently asked questions

Why convert CSV to XLSX at all?

A real workbook preserves types: numbers sort numerically, dates sort chronologically, and you can add formulas, filters and formatting. Double-clicking a CSV often mangles all of that — the workbook approach does not.

Will my leading zeros survive?

Yes. ID and zip-code style values are written as text cells where appropriate so 00123 does not collapse to 123 like Excel's raw CSV import tends to do.

Which delimiters are supported?

Comma, semicolon and tab are auto-detected, and RFC-4180 quoted fields — including embedded commas and line breaks — are parsed correctly.

Is the file uploaded during conversion?

No. Your CSV is parsed and the XLSX is written entirely in your browser by SheetJS. No server involvement at any step.
