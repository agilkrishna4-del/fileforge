Convert Word documents into tidy HTML or plain text while keeping headings, lists and tables.

About the DOCX Converter

Open a Word document without Word — and without uploading it. This converter parses .docx files locally and extracts clean, structured content: headings keep their hierarchy, lists stay lists, tables survive with their cells intact, and bold/italic formatting is preserved in the HTML output. Get plain text when you just need the words.

Under the hood it uses mammoth, a focused DOCX reader trusted for exactly this job. Because parsing happens in your browser, confidential contracts, HR letters, drafts and legal documents are never emailed to a converter server. Copy the result, download it as a file, or feed it wherever you need clean content.

Frequently asked questions

Does this convert Word to PDF?

Not directly — this tool focuses on extracting clean, structured content as HTML or text. That output pastes cleanly into editors, CMSs and email clients, which covers most real-world conversion needs.

Are images in the document converted?

Embedded images can be included in the HTML output as data URIs. For a lightweight text-focused export you can strip them — the preview shows exactly what you will get.

Will the layout look exactly like Word?

No — and that is deliberate. The goal is clean, semantic content rather than a pixel copy of Word's layout. Fonts and page margins are dropped; meaning and structure are kept.

Is my document uploaded?

Never. The .docx is unzipped and parsed by JavaScript in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.
