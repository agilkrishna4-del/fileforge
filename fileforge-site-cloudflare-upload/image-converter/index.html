About the Image Converter

Convert any image between PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP and GIF without installing software or uploading a single byte. The converter re-encodes each picture locally with the browser's own image engine, so a batch of screenshots, photos or graphics converts in seconds. Pick a target format, optionally resize, and download the results immediately.

Format conversion is one of those chores that sends people to sketchy upload sites. This tool keeps the job on your machine: ideal for turning WebP downloads into JPG for older software, converting PNG photos into much smaller WebP for the web, or producing universally compatible JPG copies of graphics. No accounts, no watermarks, no daily caps.

Frequently asked questions

Which formats can I convert between?

PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, BMP and GIF. Any of these can be converted to any other — for example WebP to JPG, PNG to WebP, or JPG to PNG.

Why convert to WebP?

WebP files are typically 25–35% smaller than equivalent JPGs at the same visual quality, which makes websites load faster. All modern browsers support WebP; use JPG only when targeting very old software.

Does converting lose quality?

Moving to a lossless format (PNG, WebP lossless) preserves everything. Moving to JPG re-encodes the image once at your chosen quality — at 85% or above the difference is invisible in practice.

Can I convert a whole folder at once?

Yes — drop as many files as you like. Each one is converted independently in your browser, and you can download the entire batch in one go.
