About the PDF Merger

Combine any number of PDF files into one clean document — entirely inside your browser. Add files, drag them into the order you want, and merge: pdf-lib stitches the pages together locally, preserving text, fonts, images and vector quality. No re-compression, no quality loss, no 'free tier' page limits.

Merging is the most common PDF chore there is — invoices for an expense report, chapters of a report, scans of a signed contract. Uploading any of those to a random website is an unnecessary risk. Here the whole job happens on your device: there is no upload step at all, so confidential documents stay confidential.

Frequently asked questions

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

As many as you like. Because merging runs locally with pdf-lib, there is no server-imposed file count or page limit — only your device's memory.

Will merging reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied directly into the new document without re-compression, so text remains selectable and images keep their original resolution.

Can I reorder files after adding them?

Yes — arrange documents in any sequence before merging, and the combined PDF follows that order page by page.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

Never. The merge happens in your browser tab. Open the network inspector and you will see zero file transfers — that is the whole point of the tool.
