About the PDF Page Editor

Fix the two most common PDF annoyances in one place: wrong page orientation and pages that should not be there. The editor shows every page as a thumbnail in a grid — rotate any page in 90° steps, tick the ones to delete, and apply. A corrected document is generated locally with pdf-lib, keeping everything else untouched.

It is the tool for tidying scans: straighten the pages your scanner turned sideways, drop the blank trailing page, remove the duplicate — and never send the document to an online editor that stores it. Your PDF is read, edited and written entirely on your device.

How to use it

  1. 1

    Open your PDF

    Drop the file in — page thumbnails render in a grid immediately.

  2. 2

    Rotate what is crooked

    Click rotate on any page to turn it in 90° steps until it reads correctly.

  3. 3

    Mark pages for deletion

    Select the pages you no longer need — blanks, duplicates, drafts.

  4. 4

    Apply and download

    Generate the cleaned document locally and save it. The original stays on your disk unmodified.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate a page in a PDF?

Open the file, click the rotate control on the offending page (each click turns it 90°), then apply. The exported PDF keeps that page in its new orientation permanently.

How do I delete pages from a PDF?

Select the unwanted pages in the thumbnail grid, apply the edit, and download the new file. Everything you did not select is preserved exactly.

Will editing change the rest of my document?

No. Only the pages you rotate or remove are affected; all other pages, text and images are copied through unchanged.

Does this tool upload my PDF?

It cannot — there is no server. Thumbnails come from pdf.js and the edited file is written by pdf-lib, both inside your browser.
