Frequently asked questions

Can I convert MOV to MP4?

Yes — iPhone and camera MOV files convert cleanly to universally playable MP4 with H.264 video, ready for any device, editor or platform.

How do I turn a video into a GIF?

Drop the clip, choose GIF, set the width and frame rate (10–15 fps keeps files small), and convert. Trimming first in a video editor keeps GIF size sensible.

How do I extract MP3 audio from a video?

Choose MP3 as the output and convert — the audio track is extracted and encoded locally. Great for lectures, interviews and music videos.

Does converting upload my file?

No. FFmpeg.wasm decodes and encodes the video inside your browser. The only network activity after page load is the one-time engine download.
